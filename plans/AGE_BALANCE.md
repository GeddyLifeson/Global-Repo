# AGE_BALANCE: per-Age coverage, thin Ages, and fixes (2026-11-03)

Requested by Marlon 11-03: "how far out are we from everything, and can we add anything so each Age feels as robust as the others?" Ages: I Ancient, II Ailean, III Holy, IV Sundering, V Strangers, VI Kingdom, VII Dubhan, VIII Future, RODOS (all real time, Civ tree only for eras and Civilopedia).

Files: `plans/AGE_BALANCE_MATRIX.csv` (37 rows x 9 Ages, regenerated by `age_balance.py`, S6916), `plans/AGE_BALANCE_NODES.json` (19 proposed progression nodes plus 15 wires, checked against the real checker).

## 1. Method and limits (read first)
- Sources: `plans/progression/graph.json` (199 nodes, 11-02), `era-index/tech_ages_by_item_unmerged.csv` (52,982 mod items by Age; MOD items only, so Panscriptum-built weapons, NPCs, rites and quests are NOT in it), and the 12 `era-index/feeds/*.json` owner feeds (10-27 to 11-01).
- A cell is "thin" when it is under 50% of that row's median over Ages I-VII. By-design zeros are not counted (Age I has no vehicles or upgrades; Earth has nothing dated past VII).
- Mobs, bosses and vehicle counts per Age have no feed. I used graph nodes and the plans (ASCENDANT, MEGAMACHINES, SHIPBUILDING) for those, so they are weaker evidence than the item and feed counts.
- Item counts are decor-heavy (Chisel alone is 9,213 in Age II). I split them by function; `items:decor` is excluded from the thin count.

## 3. Which Ages are thin (count of thin cells, most to least)
1. **VIII Future (15) and RODOS (15).** Both lack whole systems. VIII has no farm, magic, econ, settlement, culture or upgrade node, one combat node, only 12 structures and 70 textures. RODOS has no creature, food or store node, 4 structures, 41 vendor rows, 89 textures, and its only mod items are Allthemodium ores. Caveat: VIII and RODOS gear sits in the library gear chain (`combat.gear_a8`, `gear_a9`) and the Panscriptum threads, which the item table does not see, so "gear 0" is overstated; the missing systems are real.
2. **V Strangers (9).** Zero magic and zero food nodes, 30 structures, 8 magic items, 22 gear items. It is a machine Age only (Modern Industrialization, Railcraft, Project Red, 2,183 machine items) and feels like a different game to a magic or farming player.
3. **II Ailean (8).** 16 nodes, no economy node, 1 magic item, 19 gear items, 55 domesticable animals, 52 vendor rows, 37 clothing rows. Its 19,500 items are almost all Chisel and tree variants (decor).
4. **VII Dubhan (6).** Storage Age (AE2, Refined Storage, Cable Tiers = 2,090) and the boss Age, but 66 machine items, 4 farm items, no creature items, 40 structures.
5. **I Ancient (5).** Strong corpora (Codex, Earth, Library, animals). Thin on usable tools: 10 machine items, no vessel node (ships and trans zero), 84 vendors.
6. VI Kingdom (4: 14 structures, 8 magic, 16 farm). 7. III Holy (2) and IV Sundering (2) are the benchmark Ages.

Cross-cutting: Codex Civilopedia is 34,059 in Age I against about 800 to 2,000 in II to VII (a data skew, not missing design: Age I got the bulk generation). Bosses cluster at VII and RODOS (`combat.bosses`, nine Ascendant bosses); Ages I to VI have no boss node.

### C. Work for existing lanes (not new threads)
- **Textures VIII and RODOS** (70 and 89 against 4,000 to 6,000): add to the texture lane via a compute job. The Age VIII and RODOS items must have 64 px art before they ship.

- **Age Wardens**: one mini-boss per Age I to VI in `diaithir-bosses` plus a Warden for VIII, wired to the existing rite and quest framework. This is the biggest design add; the 6 base bosses and 9 Ascendant bosses stay where they are.
- **Codex Civilopedia II to VII**: Library-to-content thread (`...5hZxgw1DMDAKuvBtRvZtDA`) raises each to about 3,000 from real Library text only.
- **Age II and V gear**: the 19 and 22 gear items are mod-only; confirm the Panscriptum weapons thread covers both before adding anything.
